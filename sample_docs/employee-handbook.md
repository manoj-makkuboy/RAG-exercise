# Lumen Labs Employee Handbook

*Fictional company, written as sample data for DocChat.*

## Working hours and remote work

Our core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 CET, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you
can organise your day as you like. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week.
Fully remote arrangements need written approval from your manager and the People team.

Team members who work remotely from another country for more than 30 days in a calendar year
must notify the People team in advance, because this can have tax and insurance consequences.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid vacation per year, plus public holidays in their
country of employment. Part-time employees receive vacation pro rata to their contracted hours.
Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire
on 31 March.

Vacation requests of more than 10 consecutive working days should be submitted at least
six weeks in advance.

## Sick leave

If you are ill, tell your manager before 10:00 on your first sick day. From the fourth
consecutive sick day you need a doctor's note. Sick days do not count against vacation.

## Parental leave

Lumen Labs offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all parents, regardless of gender,
to be taken within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption of a child. This is in
addition to any statutory leave.

## Learning budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 EUR for courses, conferences, books and
certifications. Unused budget does not carry over. Requests above 500 EUR need manager
approval through the Learning form in the HR portal.

## Equipment

New employees choose between a 14-inch or 16-inch laptop. Laptops are replaced every three
years. Each employee also receives a one-time home office allowance of 750 EUR for a desk,
chair or monitor.

## Expenses

Submit expenses within 60 days through the Expenses app with a photo of the receipt. Meals
while travelling are reimbursed up to 60 EUR per day. Economy class is the default for flights
under six hours.

## Probation and notice periods

The probation period is six months, with a two-week notice period on both sides. After
probation, the notice period is two months to the end of a calendar month.
