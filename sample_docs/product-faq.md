# Lumen Notes – Product FAQ

*Fictional product, written as sample data for DocChat.*

## What is Lumen Notes?

Lumen Notes is a collaborative note-taking app for teams. Notes sync across web, macOS,
Windows, iOS and Android. Offline edits are merged automatically when the device reconnects.

## Plans and pricing

| Plan       | Price                     | Members   | Storage per member |
|------------|---------------------------|-----------|--------------------|
| Free       | 0 EUR                     | up to 3   | 2 GB               |
| Team       | 8 EUR per member / month  | unlimited | 50 GB              |
| Enterprise | custom pricing            | unlimited | 1 TB               |

Annual billing gives a 20% discount on the Team plan. Enterprise includes SSO (SAML),
audit logs, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

## Free trial

New workspaces get a 14-day free trial of the Team plan. No credit card is needed. When the
trial ends, the workspace moves to the Free plan unless someone upgrades.

## Refunds

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time and are not refunded for the current
month. Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase; after that,
refunds are pro rata for the remaining full months. Refunds are paid to the original payment
method within 10 business days.

## Data export and deletion

Workspace admins can export all notes as Markdown or PDF from Settings → Export. When a
workspace is deleted, data is kept for 30 days so it can be restored, then permanently
erased from all systems, including backups, within a further 60 days.

## Where is my data stored?

Customer data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt, with a replica in Dublin). Enterprise customers
can request US data residency.

## Integrations

Lumen Notes integrates with Slack, Google Drive, Jira and GitHub. A public REST API is
available on Team and Enterprise plans, limited to 600 requests per minute per workspace.

## Support

Free plan users get community support. Team plans get email support with a first response
within one business day. Enterprise plans get 24/7 support with a one-hour first-response
target for critical incidents.
